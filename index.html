WhoFedLOL

The best League of Legends stream clips, every week.

WhoFedLOL is a YouTube channel that publishes compilations of the most watched League of Legends clips from English-speaking Twitch streamers, plus short vertical clips for YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Instagram.

The videos are assembled and uploaded by ClipForge, a personal tool run by the channel owner on their own server. ClipForge is not offered to anyone else.

Credits and removal requests

Every clip belongs to its streamer. Each video credits the streamers it features and links to their channels. If you are a streamer and want your clips removed, write to damiancb97@gmail.com and they will be taken down.
